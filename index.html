Undead Warlock: Complete Guide

A practical guide to leveling, choosing a demon, managing resources, and helping your group. WoW Forever changes parts of Classic, so check current in-game tooltips whenever they differ from the static values here.

What Does a Warlock Do?

Warlocks are ranged damage dealers who combine Shadow and Fire damage, damage-over-time effects (DoTs), curses, drain spells, and a controllable demon. Trade health for mana with Life Tap, recover with drain spells, and create Healthstones and Soulstones using Soul Shards.

Core loop: choose the right demon → let it establish threat when you need safety → apply a DoT/Bane and a useful Curse → use a wand or efficient spell as filler → drain or Life Tap as needed. Finish suitable targets with Drain Soul to collect shards.

Why Play Undead?

Undead, also called Forsaken, are a Horde race available to Warlocks. Their racials complement the class's control and sustain: Will of the Forsaken helps against some crowd-control effects, Cannibalize can restore health from eligible corpses, and Shadow Resistance provides defense.

Racial abilities and their exact effects may have changed in Forever. Check in-game tooltips for eligible targets, duration, and cooldown; do not assume Classic values still apply.

Leveling 1–60: Key Milestones

1–9 · Learn the Basics

Use your Imp, learn DoT timing, and start collecting Soul Shards. Corruption, Life Tap, and Bane of Agony arrive early.

10–19 · Voidwalker

Complete the demon quest. The Voidwalker makes solo play safer; let it build threat before dealing heavy damage. A wand reduces mana use.

20–29 · More Tools

Unlock the Sayaad and expand your Curse/Bane options. Choose between safety, control, and damage instead of using the same pet for every pull.

30–39 · Drains and Utility

Siphon Life and the Felhunter improve sustain and group utility. Spell Lock unlocks after the Felhunter itself; check your spellbook.

40–49 · Channeled Damage

Wrack is a channel: use it when you can stand still and the target will live long enough. Keep using Felsteed and your control tools.

50–60 · Prepare for Your Role

Learn late-game spells and prepare group tools, shards, and consumables. Build for the content you play; a leveling priority is not automatically a raid or PvP build.

Use the level slider and the Spells & Rotation, Talents, and Gear & Quests tabs for level-specific details.

Choose a Demon for the Job

Imp: early damage and a group buff; watch its mana.

Voidwalker: soloing and safer pulls; let it establish threat.

Succubus/Incubus: Seduce controls eligible Humanoid targets; do not control targets your group needs to attack.

Felhunter: dispel and interrupt utility against magic and casters once the right abilities are learned.

Pet damage, threat, and available abilities can change in Forever. Check your demon's spellbook and tooltips.

How to Help Your Group

  • Ask which Curse your group needs; do not stack Curses that replace one another on the same target.
  • Use Banish on the right Demon or Elemental, Fear carefully around adds, and Seduce only when the group can maintain control.
  • Create Healthstones, keep a Soulstone ready when the group wants a resurrection plan, and save shards for Ritual of Summoning.
  • Switch demons for the encounter: a tank pet is not always best in dungeons, raids, or PvP.

Gear, Talents, and Resources

Gear

Compare the whole item. Spell Power increases damage; Hit reduces misses; Intellect expands your mana pool; Stamina gives you a buffer for Life Tap and incoming damage. Spirit's value depends on Forever's Life Tap rules.

Talents

Affliction is a natural leveling choice for DoT and drain play. Adjust points for the current server tree and your level. The interactive planner is a guide aid, not a live-synced or guaranteed meta build.

Soul Shards

Bring shards for Healthstones, Soulstones, demon summons, and group summons. Use Drain Soul on suitable targets and avoid filling your bags with shards you do not need.

Affliction leveling

Talents

41 Affliction / 7 Demonology / 3 Destruction at 60; no respec. Improved Corruption early, then drain talents, Siphon Life and Wrack.

Spell priority

  • Send your demon; pull with Bane of Agony, then Corruption.
  • Use Immolate on longer-lived targets; wand until level 30.
  • From 30, Drain Life sustains you. Add Siphon Life at 37.
  • Use Wrack when health and mana are comfortable; Life Tap when health exceeds mana.

Demonology leveling

Talents

Level with Affliction talents through 26, then respec at 27. The level-60 route is 31 Demonology / 20 Affliction.

Spell priority

  • Send your demon and use Searing Pain to keep Demonic Brand active.
  • Apply Bane of Agony and a useful Curse; add Immolate to sturdy targets.
  • Drain Life for sustain. At 40, sacrifice the Imp and summon Sayaad for the Shadow setup.
Zephan's leveling guide

Destruction leveling

Talents

31 Destruction / 17 Affliction / 3 Demonology at 60. It bursts well in dungeons but spends more mana while soloing.

Spell priority

  • Keep Immolate on sturdy targets; use Conflagrate when it is active.
  • Shadow Bolt is the filler before 40; Incinerate replaces it at 40.
  • Use Shadowburn to finish targets, and Life Tap between pulls.

Stats to Compare While Leveling

1. Spell Power
Increases spell damage. Shadow damage matters for Affliction, but compare the item's full stat budget.
2. Spell Hit
Reduces misses. Suppression grants Hit to Affliction spells, so adjust your gear for your talent points.
3. Critical Strike
DoTs can critically strike in Forever; Pandemic makes Crit more valuable than in Classic.
4 Intellect · 5 Spirit · 6 Haste · 7 Stamina
Spirit grants more mana from Life Tap. Current beta data says Haste does not affect DoT or channel ticks. Stamina mainly improves survivability.

Gear to Craft and Find

Low-Level: What You Can Wear

Level 5 — Brown Linen Robe: Tailoring 30, 3 Bolt of Linen Cloth + 1 Coarse Thread; 19 armor, +1 Spirit. A cheap starter chest; replace it when better caster stats drop.

Level 6 — Cloudy Stormsewn Handwraps: Tailoring 55, 4 Bolt of Linen Cloth + 4 Coarse Thread; 13 armor, +1 Spirit. A cheap glove upgrade if your current pair is worse.

Level 10 — Novice Arcanist's Sash: Tailoring 50, 8 Bolt of Linen Cloth + 2 Fine Thread + 2 Minor Mana Potion; +4 Spell Power, +3 Intellect, and +3 Stamina. A standout early Warlock craft; requires level 10 and is BoP.

Shadow Tailoring: Recipe Progression

Look for Shadow Boots (60 Tailoring), Shadow Gloves (75), Shadow Sash (85), Shadow Circlet (100), Shadow Gown (110), and Shadow Leggings (125). These requirements are Tailoring skill, not character level.

Merchant's Favor, Horde

Merchant's Favor patterns cost 30 Favor each, and Shadow pieces are bind-on-pickup: you must have Tailoring to use them. Fill Waylaid Supply Crates and buy patterns from Jim'bek at the supply camp outside Crossroads. Check skill requirements and item stats in-game.

Dungeon Craft: Filigreed Shadow Gown

Look for Pattern: Filigreed Shadow Gown from Lord Cobrahn in Wailing Caverns. The recipe is listed at 75 Tailoring. Check the pattern and loot in the dungeon; drop rates and binding rules can change.

Wands and Other Auction House Gear

Look for Gravestone Scepter (Blackfathom Villainy, around level 18+), Staff/Orb of Soran'ruk, and new Forever dungeon weapons. Wands do not scale with Spell Power; stand still for 0.5 seconds before shooting. Prioritize Spell Power, Hit/Crit based on talents, Intellect, and Spirit; compare in-game tooltips.

Tailoring + Enchanting by Character Level

Character level unlocks profession ranks; recipes have separate skill requirements. Craft counts are estimates because yellow/green recipes may grant fewer skill-ups.

Forever 1–300 · Horde

Character Levels 1–9 · Apprentice, Skill 1–75

Tailoring: skill 1–30 Bolt of Linen Cloth (about 60 Linen Cloth), 30–55 Linen Belt (about 28), 55–75 Bolt of Woolen Cloth (about 135 Wool Cloth). Save the bolts for the next step.

Enchanting: skill 1 Runed Copper Rod; 2–20 Enchant Bracer - Inferior Stamina; 20–70 Enchant Bracer - Minor Deflect; 70–75 Enchant Chest - Minor Absorption. Horde trainers: Vance Undergloom in Tirisfal Glades or a city trainer in Undercity.

Character Levels 10–19 · Journeyman, Skill Cap 150

Rank: train Journeyman at character level 10 and skill 50; the cap becomes 150. Both professions have Horde city trainers.

Tailoring: skill 80–95 Woolen Cape, 95–100 Double-Stitched Woolen Shoulders, 100–120 Bolt of Silk Cloth, 120–140 Gloves of Meditation, and 140–150 Bolt of Mageweave if cloth is affordable. Shadow recipes start at skill 60: Boots, Gloves, Sash, Circlet, Gown, and Leggings at 60/75/85/100/110/125; they cost 30 Merchant's Favor and are BoP.

Enchanting: 75–109 Minor Absorption, 109–110 Runed Silver Rod, 110–150 Enchant 2H Weapon - Lesser Intellect. Buy materials only through the next color range.

Character Levels 20–34 · Expert, Skill Cap 225

Rank: train Expert at character level 20 and skill 125. Horde Tailoring trainer Josef Gregorian is in Undercity; Enchanting trainers with a 225 cap include one in Stonetalon Mountains.

Tailoring: 150–170 Crimson Silk Vest, 170–190 Crimson Silk Pantaloons, 190–215 Black Mageweave Gloves, and 215–225 Mageweave Bag. Warlocks can look for Pattern: Filigreed Shadow Gown from Lord Cobrahn in Wailing Caverns (75 Tailoring).

Enchanting: 150–151 Runed Golden Rod, 151–165 Minor Mana Oil, 165–185 Enchant Boots - Lesser Agility, 185–200 Enchant Bracer - Strength, 200–201 Runed Truesilver Rod, 201–220 Lesser Wizard Oil, and 220–225 Greater Defense on cloak.

Character Levels 35–60 · Artisan, Skill Cap 300

Rank: train Artisan at level 35 and skill 200. Horde Tailoring trainer Daryl Stack is in Tarren Mill; Artisan Enchanting trainer Annora is inside Uldaman.

Tailoring: 225–250 Black Mageweave Vest, 250–265 Gilded Sandals if Merchant's Favor and materials justify the cost, 265–267 Runecloth Cloak, 267–295 Runecloth Cuffs, and 295–300 Venomspew Footpads. Skill-ups after 225 vary with recipe color.

Enchanting: 225–266 Enchant Gloves - Agility, 266–277 Enchant Chest - Superior Stamina, and 277–300 Enchant Shield - Greater Stamina. Disenchant only items that are not worth selling or wearing.

Horde note: Tailoring/Enchanting Merchant's Favor patterns are sold at the camp near Crossroads. Fill Waylaid Supply Crates. Check skill, recipe color, binding, and material prices in-game before large purchases.
